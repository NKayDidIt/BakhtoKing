THE KING OF PASHTO COMEDY & VIRAL CONTENT

Bakhti Rehman (often known as Bakhti Kotak) Bakhti Rehman is a viral Pashto creator and comedian from Swat, Pakistan. Known across YouTube, TikTok, and Facebook for his expressive voice and village-life skits—often alongside Zeba Gull—his work spans quick comedy clips, improvised chats, and emotional stories for Pashtun audiences worldwide.

His authentic humor and signature catchphrases continue to make him one of the region's most beloved digital entertainers.
